Buldak Carbonara

A Product That Lets You Experience the Perfect Chemistry Between Rich, Creamy Sauce and Fiery Original Buldak Sauce

Product Information

Flavour: Carbonara

Spice Level: 2

Type: Stir-fried noodles

How to Prepare

Classic Recipe

Additional Ingredients

  • No additional ingredients

Steps

  • Boil 600 ml of water
  • Add the noodles and cook for 5 minutes
  • Drain the water, leaving approximately 8 tablespoons
  • Add the sauce and seasoning powder
  • Eat

Extra Cheesy Recipe

Additional Ingredients

  • 30 g of mozzarella
  • 15 g of grated parmesan

Steps

  • Prepare the Buldak Carbonara following the classic recipe
  • Add the mozzarella while the noodles are still hot
  • Mix until the cheese melts
  • Sprinkle with grated parmesan
  • Eat

Creamy Egg

Additional Ingredients

  • 1 egg
  • 1 tablespoon of milk

Steps

  • Prepare the Buldak Carbonara following the classic recipe
  • Beat the egg with the milk in a bowl
  • Cook the egg mixture gently in a pan until fully cooked
  • Add the cooked egg to the noodles
  • Eat.
