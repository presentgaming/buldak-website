Buldak Cheese

A Rich and Creamy Cheese Flavour Combined with the Bold Heat of Original Buldak Sauce!

Product Information

Flavour: Cheese

Spice Level: Medium

Type: Stir-fried noodles

How to Prepare

Classic Recipe

Additional Ingredients

  • No additional ingredients

Steps

  • Boil 600 ml of water
  • Add the noodles and cook for 5 minutes
  • Drain the water, leaving approximately 8 tablespoons
  • Add the sauce
  • Add the cheese powder and mix
  • Eat

Extra Cheesy Cheese

Additional Ingredients

  • 2 slices of cheddar cheese
  • 30 ml of milk

Steps

  • Prepare the Buldak Cheese following the classic recipe
  • Pour the milk into a pan over low heat
  • Add the cheddar cheese and stir until melted
  • Add the noodles and mix everything together
  • Eat

Cheese Beef

Additional Ingredients

  • any amount of ground beef you want
  • 2 teaspoons of garlic power
  • 1 teaspoon of butter

Steps

  • Prepare the Buldak Cheese following the classic recipe
  • Cook the ground beef
  • Add the soy sauce and garlic powder
  • Cook until it's done
  • Add the noodles to the pan
  • Mix everything
  • Eat
