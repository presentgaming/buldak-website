Buldak Original

A Spicy Recipe with Endless Possibilities for Delicious Combinations with Other Toppings!

Product Information

Flavour: Hot Chicken

Spice Level: 4

Type: Stir-fried noodles

How to Prepare

Classic Recipe

Additional Ingredients

  • No additional ingredients

Steps

  • Boil 600 ml of water.
  • Add the noodles and cook for 5 minutes
  • Drain the water, leaving approximately 8 tablespoons
  • Add the sauce
  • Add the sesame and seaweed flakes
  • Eat

Garlic Original

Additional Ingredients

  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 teaspoon of butter

Steps

  • Prepare the Buldak Original following the classic recipe
  • Mince the garlic clove
  • Melt the butter in a pan with low-heat
  • Add the minced garlic to the butter
  • Add the noodles and sauce to the pan
  • Eat

Honey Soy Original

Additional Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon of honey
  • 1 teaspoon of soy sauce

Steps

  • Prepare the Buldak Original noodles
  • In a small bowl, mix the honey and soy sauce
  • Add the Buldak sauce to the noodles and stir well
  • Pour in the honey and soy sauce mixture
  • Mix everything together
  • Eat
